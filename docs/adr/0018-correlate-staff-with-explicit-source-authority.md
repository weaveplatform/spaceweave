# Correlate staff with explicit source authority

Keep stable internal staff identities linked to Entra and Workday through a
unique organisation-approved identifier, retaining both source identities and
holding ambiguous matches for review. Entra owns sign-in/account security,
Workday owns workforce lifecycle, reporting structure and usual office, and
SpaceWeave owns booking preferences and dated travel choices. This prevents
mutable email addresses and competing feed updates from silently relinking
people or overriding the source responsible for a field.
