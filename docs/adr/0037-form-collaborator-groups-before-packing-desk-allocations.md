# Form collaborator groups before packing desk allocations

Allocation proceeds in two phases: form date-specific collaborator groups,
including groups of one, then pack those groups into available desks while
respecting member-specific mandatory requirements and required parking.
Pursue the best allocation under the agreed priorities, evaluating sitting
together before minimum walking distance and publishing the completed,
validated plan together; computation cost is a consequence, and unequal
placement quality is expected.
Keeping grouping and packing explicit preserves the intended collaboration
model, while completed-plan publication prevents intermediate confirmations
from restricting subsequent placement improvements.

[Supporting research](../research/two-phase-desk-allocation.md) compares
technology candidates, evidence limits and required validation without selecting
an algorithm, solver or repository architecture.
This refines the publication semantics of
[allocation cycles and group consistency](0014-use-explicit-allocation-cycles-and-atomic-group-placement.md)
and retains [Booking's planning ownership](0027-keep-attendance-planning-inside-booking.md).
