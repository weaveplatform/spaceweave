# Model neighbourhoods as flexible space bundles

A neighbourhood is a configurable bundle of desks and optional meeting rooms
within one building, and may span floors within that building. Groups are assigned
to neighbourhoods, multiple groups can use a neighbourhood on different days, and
neighbourhood sizes can flex with demand. This allows undersubscribed and
oversubscribed neighbourhoods to balance capacity as groups take their office
turns.

Keep a configured baseline bundle and apply dated adjustments that move specific,
unallocated desks between neighbourhoods. Preserve existing bookings while
balancing spare capacity against unmet demand.
