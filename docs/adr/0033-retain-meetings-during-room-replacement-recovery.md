# Retain meetings during room replacement recovery

An ordinary meeting whose replacement request fails after the previous room is
lost retains its meeting and attendees with a visible no-secured-room outcome;
notify the organiser and offer available replacements or explicit cancellation.
Booking owns the room intention and claim outcome, Exchange reconciles provider
state, and a replacement confirms only after acceptance and capacity validation.
This preserves meeting continuity at the cost of an explicit room-recovery
workflow.
