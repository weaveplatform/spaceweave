# Allocate advance desk intents together

Staff choose their attendance dates and submit desk intent within a configurable
advance planning window. Process those intents together on an independently
configured cadence, form groups based on team proximity, and assign the groups to
neighbourhoods across available floors. Considering demand together allows team
proximity to guide placement across the available capacity.

Form allocation groups separately for each attendance date from the staff who
selected that date, including groups of one; then pack those groups into the
available desks. The [two-phase allocation decision](0037-form-collaborator-groups-before-packing-desk-allocations.md)
records the grouping/packing distinction and completed-plan publication.

Team proximity is a preference: teams may be split across floors when that lets
more people receive desks. The scheduled team-based allocation workflow applies
to desks; other space kinds have booking rules suited to each kind.

A successful completed plan creates confirmed bookings together. Staff are notified
and can cancel their bookings. A desk is allocated when assigned by the system;
the assigned staff member's check-in marks it taken.
