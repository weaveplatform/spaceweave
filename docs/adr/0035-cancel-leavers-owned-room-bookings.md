# Cancel leavers' owned room bookings

A confirmed effective leaver instruction cancels the organiser's remaining
meeting-room bookings, including recurring meetings, alongside the existing
future personal desk/parking cleanup; completed history follows retention policy.
Ownership determines the target, so a departing PA's bookings for other staff
remain with those owners.
This replaces assisted room handover with automatic cancellation and visible
provider reconciliation, while Events retains its separate handover workflow.
