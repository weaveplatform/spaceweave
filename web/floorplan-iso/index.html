    How it's made

    Each source plan (this one: source) is a flat image. Its walls, doors, glazing and furniture were traced by hand into metres, calibrated against the plan's own dimension lines or scale bar.

    Every object is a footprint pushed up into a prism. Each frame, the prisms are projected through an orbiting parallel camera and ordered by separating axis so near things paint over far ones. D3 then joins the visible faces to SVG paths and handles zoom, transitions and colour.

    Hover a room for its area. Click to fly to it. Scroll or drag to zoom and pan.
